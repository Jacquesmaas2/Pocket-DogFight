# Art Direction

## Style
Simple colourful 2D cartoon graphics. Tiny toy-like ships, not realistic spacecraft.

## Ships
Strong silhouettes, bright player colours, minimal details, exaggerated engines and obvious weapon direction.

## Space
Very dark blue background, subtle stars and restrained nebula/environment detail.

## VFX
Bright lasers, glowing engines, trails, sparks, debris, screen shake and comically oversized explosions.

## Principle
Small ship. Comically large explosion.

## Camera
Top-down. Enough arena visibility to anticipate opponents. Camera effects must not harm mobile aiming.
