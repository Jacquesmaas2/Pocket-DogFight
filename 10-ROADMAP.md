# Development Roadmap

## Phase 0 - Foundation
Godot project, folder structure, input map, base arena, bootstrap.

## Phase 1 - Flying
Thrust, rotation, momentum, camera and desktop controls. Flying must feel fun.

## Phase 2 - Combat
Laser, projectiles, collisions, damage, health and explosions.

## Phase 3 - Multiplayer Foundation
Host/join, spawning, network identity and ship synchronization.

## Phase 4 - Multiplayer Combat
Authoritative damage, network projectiles, death, respawn and scoring.

## Phase 5 - Match
Countdown, timer, scoreboard, ending and rematch.

## Phase 6 - Mobile
Touch controls, HUD, landscape layout and device testing.

## Phase 7 - Game Juice
Particles, trails, screen shake, sounds, explosions and hit feedback.

## Phase 8 - Missiles
Secondary weapon, homing, ammo and warnings.

## Phase 9 - Arena
Asteroids, spawning, boundaries and environmental visuals.

## Phase 10 - Power-ups
Shield, rapid fire, missile pickup and boost.

## Phase 11 - Polish
Menus, player names, settings, audio settings and visual polish.

## Phase 12 - Online Deployment
Select and implement production multiplayer hosting. Do not commit to a provider before this phase.
