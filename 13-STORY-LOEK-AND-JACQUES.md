# Story - Loek & Jacques

## Tagline
Two pilots. One questionable situation. Absolutely no plan.

## Premise
Jacques and Loek are amateur space pilots who mostly use their tiny modified ships for racing, showing off and questionable deliveries. During a routine trip they intercept a mysterious energy Core. It is extremely valuable. Half the galaxy suddenly wants it.

They have no intention of saving the universe. They mostly want everyone to stop shooting at them.

That does not happen.

## Jacques - callsign JAX
Jacques believes every problem can be solved by building something, preferably with more power, an unnecessary button and experimental technology.

### Flying style
Technical and aggressive: tight turns, clever manoeuvres, gadgets and precision shooting.

### Ship
**The Syntiq**: small, fast and ridiculously over-engineered. Its dashboard contains far more information than any sane pilot needs.

## Loek - callsign LOCO
Loek's philosophy is simpler: if they're shooting at us, shoot back. Where Jacques wants a plan, Loek prefers the largest available button.

### Flying style
Unpredictable: missiles, speed, explosions and occasional intentional ramming.

### Ship
**The Bad Idea**: nobody knows exactly where Loek obtained it. Possibly including Loek. It should probably not still be flying.

## Chapter 1 - Just Another Tuesday
Jacques and Loek race through an asteroid field because Loek claimed his ship was faster.

The opening teaches steering, thrust, shooting, boosting and asteroid avoidance.

They detect a strange signal.

Loek: "Do we need to look at that?"

Jacques: "Absolutely not."

OBJECTIVE UPDATED: INVESTIGATE THE SIGNAL

## The Core
Inside an abandoned wreck they find a glowing object with an unknown origin, energy source and purpose.

Loek: "So we're taking it?"

Jacques: "Obviously."

As soon as they retrieve it, unidentified ships arrive and the first real dogfight starts.

Loek: "Good news."

Jacques: "What?"

Loek: "We found out who owns it."

## Chapter 2 - Everybody Wants It
Several factions hunt them: chaotic Scrap Rats, Galactic Security and a mysterious well-equipped Corporation.

## Chapter 3 - The Core Wakes Up
The Core starts altering nearby space, introducing gameplay such as gravity distortions, wormholes, weapon overcharge and slow-motion zones.

Jacques wants to understand it. Loek wants to weaponize it. Both happen, mostly accidentally.

## Chapter 4 - The Truth
The Core is not a weapon. It is a key to an ancient network of dormant space gates.

Activating the network also wakes something else.

## Chapter 5 - Oh.
An enormous unidentified ship appears and the factions stop fighting each other.

Jacques: "We may have made a small mistake."

Loek: "How small?"

The ship eclipses the screen.

Jacques: "Medium."

## Finale
The final battle takes place around activated gates, asteroids, debris, capital ships and wormholes.

Jacques discovers the Core can close the gate, but somebody must reach it.

Loek: "Race you."

Jacques: "Loek..."

Loek boosts away.

FINAL OBJECTIVE: BEAT LOEK TO THE CORE

The story ends where it started: with two friends racing tiny spaceships while everything explodes around them.

## Ending
They survive. Barely. The Core disappears. Their ships are wrecked.

Loek: "Technically I won."

Jacques: "Your ship is on fire."

Loek: "Still counts."

UNKNOWN SIGNAL DETECTED.

CUT TO BLACK.

## Story integration rule
Story must support gameplay. Story missions should introduce mechanics reusable in multiplayer: asteroid racing, wormholes, gravity anomalies, missiles and power-ups.

Avoid long cutscenes, heavy lore and one-off systems that do not contribute to the core game.

## Tone
Funny, light, fast, slightly absurd, self-aware and adventurous.
