# Product Vision

## Vision
Create a tiny multiplayer space combat game that produces funny and exciting moments within seconds.

## Player fantasy
"I am an insanely skilled pilot flying a ridiculously small spaceship."

## Core pillars
- Instant fun
- Skillful movement
- Controlled chaos
- Short sessions
- Social moments worth laughing about

## Non-goals
Pocket Dogfight is not a space simulator, MMO, progression-heavy RPG, complex tactical shooter, or pay-to-win game.

## MVP success
The MVP succeeds when 2-4 players can enter the same arena and repeatedly choose to play one more match despite limited content.
