# Backlog

Ship movement, arena, laser, health, destruction, multiplayer, respawn, scoreboard, match timer and mobile controls.

## Post-MVP
### Gameplay
Missiles, boost, shields, mines, power-ups and alternative weapons.

Team Deathmatch, King of the Asteroid, Survival, Bounty, Capture the Satellite.

### Arenas
Asteroid Belt, Space Junkyard, Moon Base, Black Hole, Alien Station.

### Fun features
Ridiculous death animations, emotes, kill streak calls, revenge indicators, near-miss effects, funny pilot names and tiny space horns.
