# Gameplay Systems

## MVP systems
### Ship movement
Rotation, thrust, momentum, max velocity and damping.

### Laser
Cooldown, projectile velocity, collision, damage and impact effect.

### Missile
Ammo, target selection, basic homing, explosion and damage.

### Health and death
Damage from weapons/collisions. At zero HP: disable control, explode, register kill and respawn.

### Score
Track player, kills and deaths.

### Power-ups
Post-core candidates: Shield, Rapid Fire, Missile, Boost.

### Arena hazards
Initial: asteroids. Later: mines, black holes, solar flares, moving gates or other comic hazards.
