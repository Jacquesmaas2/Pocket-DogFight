# Game Design

## Core loop
Spawn -> Fly -> Find enemy -> Dogfight -> Collect power-ups -> Destroy or get destroyed -> Respawn -> Score -> Results -> Rematch

## Default mode
Free For All. Kill = +1 point. Winner has most points when the timer expires.

## Movement
Arcade physics with momentum, forgiving controls, thrust, steering and optional later boost.

## Combat
Primary: fast laser projectile, unlimited ammo, short cooldown.
Secondary: homing missile, limited ammo, high damage, visible warning, dodgeable.

## Health
Initial tuning target: 3 HP. Laser: 1 damage. Missile: 2 damage. Collision damage depends on impact.

## Respawn
Explosion, short delay, safe spawn, temporary spawn protection.

## Arena
Compact arenas with asteroids, debris, walls, moving obstacles and later power-ups.

## Principle
When realism conflicts with fun, fun wins.
