# Audio and VFX

## Goal
Make simple visuals feel punchy and polished.

## Required sounds
Engine, laser, impact, missile launch, missile warning, explosion, collision, power-up, countdown, win and loss.

## VFX
Thruster trail, laser trail/hit, ship damage, explosion and missile trail.

## Screen shake
Strongest on destruction, medium on missile explosions, subtle on impacts. Allow disabling later.
