# Mobile UX

## Principle
Mobile-first, landscape orientation.

## Controls
Initial concept:
- Left thumb: steering/thrust
- Right thumb: primary fire and missile

Desktop development controls:
- W/Up: thrust
- A/D: rotate
- Space: fire
- Shift: secondary weapon

## HUD
Only essentials: health, missile availability, score and timer.

## Feedback
Immediate hit feedback, damage indication, missile warning, kill confirmation and respawn countdown.
